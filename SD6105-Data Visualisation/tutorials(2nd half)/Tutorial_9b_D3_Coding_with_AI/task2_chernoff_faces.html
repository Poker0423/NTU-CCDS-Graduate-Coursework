Climate samples 2000-2099 as a 10 × 10 grid of Chernoff faces

Each face = one year (row-major, 2000 top-left to 2099 bottom-right). Hover a face for the raw values.

Face colour avg. temperature (blue → red)
Face height max. temperature
Face width min. temperature
Eye size rainfall
Eyebrow slant humidity (angry = humid)
Nose length wind speed
Smile sunshine hours (frown → smile)

All features are min-max scaled over the 100 samples. Chernoff faces are a glyph method for high-dimensional data: good for spotting similar / outlying records at a glance, weak for reading exact values (features are not equally salient).
